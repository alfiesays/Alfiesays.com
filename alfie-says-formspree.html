The right response for every review

Routine replies, handled. Important ones, human.

Not every review needs a custom-written response. Review Concierge keeps everyday acknowledgments timely and personal, while giving complex or sensitive feedback the attention of a real person.

Routine positive review

“Everyone was so kind to our dog, Luna. We had a great visit!”

Personalized routine reply

Thank you for bringing Luna in! We’re glad you both had a positive visit and appreciate you taking the time to share your experience.

Routine replies use your business-approved guidance and personalized response patterns.

Complex or sensitive feedback

“We left feeling confused about the next steps after our appointment and wished things had been explained more clearly.”

Individually crafted human response

Thank you for letting us know about your experience. We’re sorry you left feeling uncertain. Clear communication matters to our team, and we’d appreciate the opportunity to understand what happened and help address your concerns. Please contact our team directly so we can follow up with you.

Complex, negative, or sensitive responses are individually written by a real person, with clinical or other sensitive concerns referred to your team when appropriate.

Examples are illustrative. Responses are tailored to each review and your business’s approved guidance.

Good questions

Before we get started.

Who is Alfie Says for?

Alfie Says is for pet-care businesses, including veterinary clinics and animal hospitals, grooming salons, doggy daycares, boarding facilities, kennels, and other businesses that care for pets.

What’s included in the complimentary Google Reputation Snapshot?

We review one public Google Business Profile, compare visible review and profile information with up to two relevant local competitors, and provide three practical opportunities for improvement. It is a concise, one-page snapshot, not a full marketing audit. No account access is required.

What is the difference between the two plans?

Review Management gives your business a review-request workflow, monitoring, alerts, monthly reporting, and personalized response drafts for your team to review and post. Review Concierge adds managed routine replies using your approved guidance, plus individually written human responses for complex, negative, or sensitive reviews. Custom responses are available in English, Spanish, and Portuguese.

Are the review requests automated?

We set up a review-request workflow and confirm which delivery options are supported by your existing business systems. The exact automation depends on software compatibility and account permissions, which we review before onboarding.

Are all Review Concierge replies custom-written by a person?

Not every review needs a custom response. Routine reviews receive timely, personalized replies using your business-approved guidance. Complex, negative, or sensitive reviews receive individually crafted responses written by a real person. Custom human-written responses are available in English, Spanish, and Portuguese.

How many reviews will you respond to each month?

Review Concierge is designed around an agreed monthly response allowance. The current proposed plan includes up to 20 responses per month. If your business typically receives more, we can discuss an appropriate scope before you sign up.

How quickly are responses posted?

Our proposed target is within two business days for routine reviews. Reviews involving safety, privacy, or sensitive complaints are referred to your team; clinical concerns are referred to veterinary teams when applicable and may need your direction before a response is posted.

Do you guarantee a certain number of reviews?

No. Results depend on your customer experience and whether customers choose to respond. We make the process easier, but we never promise a specific number of reviews or a particular star rating.

Do you offer incentives for positive reviews?

No. Review invitations are neutral and intended to gather honest feedback from customers, regardless of whether their experience was positive or negative. We do not buy reviews, write reviews on customers’ behalf, or condition requests on a positive rating.

What happens when you receive a negative review?

We monitor feedback and follow the escalation process agreed with your business. Routine Review Concierge replies can be handled by us. Sensitive complaints are referred to your team rather than answered independently. Clinical concerns are referred to veterinary teams when applicable.

Can you help with Yelp reviews?

We can help you stay aware of feedback across your online presence. Yelp discourages businesses from asking customers for reviews, so our review-request process focuses on platforms where solicitation is permitted.

Do I have to change my current software?

Not necessarily. Before onboarding, we review your current setup and confirm which features and integrations are compatible.

Can I cancel?

Billing and cancellation terms will be provided in writing before service begins.
